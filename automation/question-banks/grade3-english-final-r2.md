# 三年級英語期末題庫：Review 2

範圍：
- 翰林 Here We Go 2 Review 2：Units 3-4 integrated review

題數：10 題

Bloom：
- 記憶：2 題
- 理解：2 題
- 應用：2 題
- 分析：2 題
- 評鑑：2 題

題型：Wayground 單選題，A-D 四選一

學習目標覆蓋：
- 整合家庭成員、外貌與職業句型
- 辨識 he/she/I 對應
- 能在短句或對話中選擇自然答案

1. Which sentence talks about family?
A. She’s my mother.
B. She’s a doctor.
C. It’s a ruler.
D. Are you a cook?
答案：A
Bloom：記憶
依據：Review 2 reviews Unit 3 family language.

2. Which sentence talks about a job?
A. He’s my brother.
B. He’s a driver.
C. She’s short.
D. Nice to meet you.
答案：B
Bloom：記憶
依據：Review 2 reviews Unit 4 jobs.

3. A: Who’s she? B: She’s my sister. She’s tall. What do we know?
A. She is my brother and short.
B. He is my father.
C. She is my sister and she is tall.
D. It is a pencil.
答案：C
Bloom：理解
依據：Combines family word and adjective.

4. A: Is he a teacher? B: No, he’s not. He’s a doctor. What is he?
A. a teacher
B. a student
C. a nurse
D. a doctor
答案：D
Bloom：理解
依據：The negative answer gives the real job.

5. Which question should you ask when pointing to your grandma in a photo?
A. Who’s he?
B. Who’s she?
C. Are you a driver?
D. What’s that?
答案：B
Bloom：應用
依據：Grandma uses she and Unit 3 Who question.

6. Your friend asks, "Are you a student?" You are a student. Choose the best answer.
A. Yes, he is.
B. No, she’s not.
C. She’s my sister.
D. Yes, I am.
答案：D
Bloom：應用
依據：Are you...? requires I answer.

7. Which dialogue is wrong?
A. A: Who’s he? B: She’s my mother.
B. A: Who’s she? B: She’s my sister.
C. A: Is he a cook? B: Yes, he is.
D. A: Are you a student? B: Yes, I am.
答案：A
Bloom：分析
依據：Who’s he? should not answer with she/mother.

8. Read: "She’s my mother. She’s a teacher." Which two ideas are included?
A. animal and size
B. school thing and color
C. family and job
D. weather and time
答案：C
Bloom：分析
依據：The sentence combines Unit 3 and Unit 4 content.

9. Which response is the most natural? A: Nice to meet you.
A. B: Nice to meet you, too.
B. B: No, he’s not.
C. B: She’s short.
D. B: It’s a book.
答案：A
Bloom：評鑑
依據：Review includes daily talk from Unit 3.

10. Which sentence best fits a picture of a tall father who is a cook?
A. She’s my mother. She’s short. She’s a nurse.
B. He’s my father. He’s tall. He’s a cook.
C. It’s my father. It’s a ruler.
D. I can draw. It’s a cook.
答案：B
Bloom：評鑑
依據：Best option integrates correct gender, family, adjective, and job.
