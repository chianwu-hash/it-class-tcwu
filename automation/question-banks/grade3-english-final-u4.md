# 三年級英語期末題庫：Unit 4 Is He a Teacher?

範圍：
- 翰林 Here We Go 2 Unit 4: Is He a Teacher?

題數：15 題

Bloom：
- 記憶：3 題
- 理解：4 題
- 應用：3 題
- 分析：3 題
- 評鑑：2 題

題型：Wayground 單選題，A-D 四選一

學習目標覆蓋：
- 辨識職業單字
- 能使用 Are you...? 與 Is he/she...? 問答
- 能依 he/she/I 判斷自然回答

1. Which word means "學生"?
A. a student
B. a teacher
C. a doctor
D. a driver
答案：A
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 4 jobs include a student.

2. Which word means "護士"?
A. a cook
B. a nurse
C. a driver
D. a teacher
答案：B
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 4 jobs include a nurse.

3. Which word means "司機"?
A. a doctor
B. a student
C. a driver
D. a cook
答案：C
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 4 jobs include a driver.

4. A: Are you a student? B: Yes, ______.
A. he is
B. she is
C. it is
D. I am
答案：D
Bloom：理解
依據：Are you...? is answered with I am.

5. A: Is he a teacher? B: No, ______ not.
A. she’s
B. he’s
C. I’m
D. you’re
答案：B
Bloom：理解
依據：Is he...? is answered with he is/he’s.

6. A: Is she a doctor? B: Yes, ______ is.
A. he
B. I
C. you
D. she
答案：D
Bloom：理解
依據：Is she...? is answered with she is.

7. Which sentence is correct?
A. He’s a cook.
B. He a cook.
C. His a cook.
D. Him is a cook.
答案：A
Bloom：理解
依據：Unit 4 uses He’s a ____.

8. 老師問你 "Are you a nurse?"，但你是學生。你應該怎麼回答？
A. Yes, he is.
B. No, she’s not.
C. No, I’m not. I’m a student.
D. It’s a student.
答案：C
Bloom：應用
依據：You questions need I answers.

9. Mia 是一位女醫生。下列哪一組問答正確？
A. A: Is she a doctor? B: Yes, she is.
B. A: Is he a doctor? B: Yes, he is.
C. A: Are you a doctor? B: Yes, she is.
D. A: Who’s she? B: It’s a doctor.
答案：A
Bloom：應用
依據：Female person uses she.

10. （提示：Rocky 是一位男司機）A: Is Rocky a cook? B: ______
A. Yes, he is. He’s a cook.
B. No, he’s not. He’s a driver.
C. No, she’s not. She’s a driver.
D. I’m a driver.
答案：B
Bloom：應用
依據：Is he...? 的否定回答需用 he，並說出正確職業。

11. 問句是 "Is he a nurse?"，下列哪一個回答正確？
A. Yes, she is.
B. Yes, I am.
C. Yes, he is.
D. It’s a nurse.
答案：C
Bloom：分析
依據：Question uses he，回答需使用 he is。

12. 下列哪一組對話的 he/she 用法錯誤？
A. A: Is she a cook? B: No, she’s not.
B. A: Are you a doctor? B: No, I’m not.
C. A: Is he a driver? B: Yes, he is.
D. A: Is he a teacher? B: Yes, she is.
答案：D
Bloom：分析
依據：he question should not be answered with she.

13. 下列哪一組問句主詞和回答主詞配對正確？
A. A: Are you a cook? B: No, he’s not.
B. A: Is he a teacher? B: Yes, he is.
C. A: Is she a nurse? B: Yes, I am.
D. A: Are you a doctor? B: Yes, she is.
答案：B
Bloom：分析
依據：分析 Are you...? 需用 I 回答，Is he...? 需用 he 回答，Is she...? 需用 she 回答。

14. Mia 是一位女司機。Peter 寫 "He’s a driver."，這句話應該怎麼判斷？
A. 正確，Mia 應該用 he。
B. 不正確，Mia 應該用 she，所以要寫 "She’s a driver."
C. 不正確，driver 前面不能用 a。
D. 正確，所有職業句子都要用 he。
答案：B
Bloom：評鑑
依據：評估同學句子是否正確，女性人物 Mia 應使用 She’s，並搭配職業單字 a driver。

15. 老師問："Is he a doctor?" 這個男孩不是醫生，他是護士。下列哪一組回答正確？
A. No, he’s not. He’s a nurse.
B. Yes, he is. He’s a nurse.
C. No, she’s not. She’s a nurse.
D. No, he’s not. He’s a teacher.
答案：A
Bloom：評鑑
依據：Unit 4 目標句型需用 Yes/No 回答 Is he...? 並可補充正確職業。
