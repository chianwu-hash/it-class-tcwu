# 三年級英語期末題庫：Unit 3 Who’s She?

範圍：
- 翰林 Here We Go 2 Unit 3: Who’s She?

題數：15 題

Bloom：
- 記憶：3 題
- 理解：4 題
- 應用：3 題
- 分析：3 題
- 評鑑：2 題

題型：Wayground 單選題，A-D 四選一

學習目標覆蓋：
- 辨識家庭成員與 tall/short
- 能用 Who’s he/she? 詢問並回答
- 能使用 Nice to meet you. 等日常用語

1. Which word means "媽媽"?
A. mother
B. brother
C. sister
D. grandfather
答案：A
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 3 family words include mother.

2. Which word means "爸爸"?
A. sister
B. father
C. grandmother
D. mother
答案：B
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 3 family words include father.

3. Which pair has opposite meanings?
A. mother / mom
B. father / dad
C. tall / short
D. grandpa / grandfather
答案：C
Bloom：記憶
依據：Unit 3 adjective pair: tall and short.

4. A: Who’s she? B: ______ my sister.
A. He’s
B. I’m
C. You’re
D. She’s
答案：D
Bloom：理解
依據：Who’s she? is answered with She’s.

5. A: Who’s he? B: ______ my father.
A. She’s
B. He’s
C. It’s
D. They’re
答案：B
Bloom：理解
依據：Who’s he? is answered with He’s.

6. Which sentence is correct?
A. He’s my mother.
B. She my mother.
C. Her’s my mother.
D. She’s my mother.
答案：D
Bloom：理解
依據：Unit 3 sentence pattern: She’s my mother.

7. A: Nice to meet you. B: ______
A. Nice to meet you, too.
B. Good job.
C. Try again.
D. I don’t know.
答案：A
Bloom：理解
依據：Unit 3 daily talk includes Nice to meet you, too.

8. Look at the sentence: "He’s my brother. He’s tall." Who is tall?
A. My sister
B. My mother
C. My brother
D. My grandmother
答案：C
Bloom：應用
依據：Students apply he/brother/tall sentence meaning.

9. A girl points to her grandma. What should she say?
A. She’s my grandmother.
B. He’s my grandfather.
C. She’s my brother.
D. He’s my father.
答案：A
Bloom：應用
依據：Grandmother uses she.

10. A boy points to his dad. Which dialogue is best?
A. A: Who’s she? B: He’s my father.
B. A: Who’s he? B: He’s my father.
C. A: What’s this? B: He’s my father.
D. A: Is he a teacher? B: He’s my father.
答案：B
Bloom：應用
依據：Unit 3 target question is Who’s he?

11. Which answer matches "Who’s she?"
A. He’s my dad. He’s tall.
B. It’s a ruler.
C. She’s my mother. She’s short.
D. Yes, she is.
答案：C
Bloom：分析
依據：Only option C matches she and the Unit 3 pattern.

12. Which sentence has a pronoun mistake?
A. She’s my grandmother.
B. He’s my brother.
C. She’s my mother.
D. He’s my sister.
答案：D
Bloom：分析
依據：Sister should use she.

13. A: Who’s he? B: He’s my grandfather. He’s short. Which word tells his height?
A. grandfather
B. short
C. he
D. my
答案：B
Bloom：分析
依據：short is the adjective describing height.

14. Which conversation is the most natural in Unit 3?
A. A: Who’s she? B: It’s a pen.
B. A: Who’s he? B: I can swim.
C. A: Who’s he? B: Yes, it is.
D. A: Who’s she? B: She’s my sister.
答案：D
Bloom：評鑑
依據：The target pattern asks about people.

15. Which sentence would you choose for a family picture of your mom?
A. She’s my mother.
B. He’s my brother.
C. It’s my eraser.
D. I can dance.
答案：A
Bloom：評鑑
依據：Best sentence for identifying mother in a family photo.
